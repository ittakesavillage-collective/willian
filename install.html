Willian

Willian's own village app. Free, no app store needed.

Open this page in your browser first

You've opened it inside another app, which can't add Willian to your home screen. Tap the ··· menu and choose Open in browser, or copy the link and paste it into your browser.

Add Willian to your phone
  1. 1

    Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right of Chrome.

  2. 2

    Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).

  3. 3

    Tap Install. Willian appears with your other apps.

Add Willian to your iPhone
  1. 1

    Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari (top right on iPad).

  2. 2

    Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.

  3. 3

    Tap Add. Willian appears on your home screen.

Opening Willian from the home screen keeps your settings safe. iPhones clear website data that isn't added to the home screen.
